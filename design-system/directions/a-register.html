Direction A · Register

Thesis. Trust is the product. Not a badge bolted onto a listing card, but the whole visual language: this is a register of vouched-for people, kept properly.

Type. Newsreader for the names of people and businesses — a serif gives a person dignity that a grotesk does not. Public Sans for everything procedural.

Structure. Ruled rows, not cards. The seal is the only circular thing on the screen, so verification is the first thing the eye finds.

Restraint. Stamp red appears on seals and nowhere else — never on a button. Spending it on a CTA would make verification decorative.
