Direction C · Two Tongues

Thesis. The product's whole reason to exist is that you should not have to operate in your second language. So neither script is ever hidden behind a toggle — both are on screen, always, at equal weight. The bilingualism is the identity.

Type. Instrument Sans set against Noto Sans KR at matched optical size, so Hangul never reads as a subtitle to the English. Two scripts, one voice.

Structure. Every name is a pair. The pairing is the layout module, repeated down the screen — which is also why there is no third colour: the type is doing all of the work.

Restraint. One blue, used only for the language you have chosen as primary. Switch to Korean and the blue moves to the Hangul.
