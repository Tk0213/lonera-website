    Who's behind Lonera

    Lonera was started by TK Lim and Connor Kim, two students in Calgary who grew up watching their own families — and neighbours from Korean, Filipino, South Asian, and other newcomer communities — struggle to find local help they could trust in a new country.

    We're not a big company — just two people trying to build something the community actually needs. Every provider on Lonera is real-ID verified before they can list, and every bigger booking is protected in escrow until the work is actually done.

    We built Lonera with deep respect for community spaces like CN Dream that have served Calgary's Korean community for decades — we're not here to replace them. Lonera is a modern, trust-first alternative for a city where roughly 1 in 3 residents is an immigrant: real ID verification, secure escrow payments, and multilingual search, open to every newcomer community in Calgary.
