Who's behind Lonera

Lonera was started by TK Lim and Connor Kim, two students in Calgary who grew up watching their own families struggle to find local help they could trust in a new country.

We're not a big company — just two people trying to build something the community actually needs. Every provider on Lonera is real-ID verified before they can list, and every bigger booking is protected until the work is actually done.
